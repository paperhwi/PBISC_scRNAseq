## 2. Manuscript Results Draft (English, Synchronized to `Fig. 7a–q` & `Supplementary Figs. 1–4`)

### Multi-modal cohort extension, pre-specified screening, and external validation of PBISC-rescued immune associations in ESKD COVID-19

To overcome sample-size and fatal-event constraints in single-cell clinical studies without duplicating overlapping donors, we integrated droplet CITE-seq (`Real`) with bulk-to-single-cell reconstruction (`PBISC`) across the Imperial College end-stage kidney disease (ESKD) haemodialysis cohort (`105` bulk PBMC RNA-seq donors: `68` COVID-19-positive and `37` COVID-19-negative; **Fig. 7a** and **Supplementary Fig. 1**). Canonical chronic lymphocytic leukaemia (CLL) markers (`ROR1` and `FMOD`) cleanly isolated the two documented benign-CLL donors excluded by Stephenson et al. (`C116` and `C139`; $\log_2(\mathrm{CPM}+1) > 4.2$ and $> 3.4$) and confirmed zero occult CLL cases among either the `37` non-CLL `Real` COVID-19 patients (`10` `Real`-only plus `27` shared `Real ∩ PBISC` bridge donors counted once via `Real`; `138` longitudinal draws, `7` deaths) or the `29` non-overlapping Wave-1 bulk-only patients reconstructed by PBISC (`PBISC new`; `79` draws, `+6` direct COVID-19 deaths; **Fig. 7a, c** and **Supplementary Fig. 2a–c**). Adding `PBISC new` expanded the non-redundant primary cohort by `+78%` (`37 → 66` patients, `217` longitudinal draws), enriched steroid-naive Wave-1 cases (`0%` dexamethasone exposure vs. `7` exposed patients / `34` draws in `Real`), and increased critical `WHO 3` patients by `+140%` (`5 → 12`), all recorded deaths including follow-up by `+86%` (`7 → 13`), acute direct/indirect COVID-19 deaths by `+120%` (`5 → 11`), and direct COVID-19 deaths by `+200%` (`3 → 9`; **Fig. 7b** and **Supplementary Fig. 1a–c**). Because paired profiling of the `27` shared bridge donors demonstrated systematic mRNA-mass versus cell-count baseline scale shifts (`B ASC IgA` median `4.61%` vs. `0.49%`, exact paired Wilcoxon $P = 7.5 \times 10^{-8}$; `HSPC` `0.48%` vs. `0.13%`, $P = 3.0 \times 10^{-4}$; **Supplementary Fig. 2i**), we pooled `Real` and `PBISC new` using within-source rank-based inverse normal scores (`Normal score`), while verifying every association in raw `% of PBMCs` (**Supplementary Fig. 2j–o**).

Screening all `43` fine-grained leukocyte subsets ($\ge 30\%$ detection in `Real`, excluding erythrocytes and platelets) against peak WHO severity (Spearman $\rho$) and mortality (Mann–Whitney $\mathrm{AUC}$) through a pre-specified five-step funnel yielded `16` Benjamini–Hochberg FDR-significant subsets in `Real + PBISC new` ($q < 0.05$ for severity or $q < 0.10$ for death), `15` with concordant effect directions in both cohorts (removing `NKT`), and `12` surviving recruitment centre/wave adjustment and exclusion of the dexamethasone-induced monocyte state (`CD14 mono dex`, `CD14 mono IFN`, and `MAIT` removed; **Fig. 7d, e** and **Supplementary Fig. 3b**). Four subsets—`NK CD56bright` ($\rho = -0.50, q = 3.7 \times 10^{-4}$), `B ASC dividing` ($\rho = +0.48, q = 6.1 \times 10^{-4}$), `CD16 mono` ($\rho = -0.40, q = 0.0062$), and `B ASC IgM` ($\rho = +0.31, q = 0.042$), together with `T CD8 CM` for severity ($\rho = -0.50, q = 3.7 \times 10^{-4}$)—were already FDR-significant in `Real` alone (`n80 = 19–24` `Real` patients) and replicated in `PBISC new` ($P_{\mathrm{het}} = 0.20\text{--}0.62$; **Fig. 7e–g, i** and **Supplementary Fig. 2d–h**). By contrast, `8` subsets required `PBISC new` to cross FDR significance; for the six followed associations, `Real` alone required `31` to `>35` (`n.r.`) patients for `80%` detection power, which `PBISC new` reduced to `≤ 10` `Real` patients (`26` for `T CD8 CM` death; **Fig. 7d–g**).

Evaluating these `8` PBISC-dependent candidates across four credibility benchmarks—cross-source homogeneity ($P_{\mathrm{het}}$), 27-shared-donor concordance, stability across `14` analytical/confounder variants, and mortality discrimination within severe/critical (`WHO 2–3`) disease (**Fig. 7h** and **Supplementary Fig. 3c**)—set aside `NK CD56dim`, `T γδ`, and `CD16 mono C1` due to weak shared-donor tracking ($\rho = 0.07\text{--}0.19$) or `PBISC new`-restricted lymphopenia (`NK CD56dim` $P_{\mathrm{het}} = 0.011$; **Supplementary Fig. 2h**) and validated five focus populations across **`B ASC`**, **`T CD8`**, and **`HSPC`** compartments (**Fig. 7h–q**):
1. **Class-switched `B ASC IgG` and `B ASC IgA` severity expansion**: Concordant effects in `Real` ($\rho = +0.36, +0.38$) and `PBISC new` ($\rho = +0.42, +0.39$; $P_{\mathrm{het}} = 0.77, 0.97$) reached robust combined FDR significance ($\rho = +0.39, q = 0.0071$ and $\rho = +0.38, q = 0.0078$; `14/14` variants; power at `20` `Real` patients `29% → 96%` and `34% → 97%`, shuffled `15–16%`; **Fig. 7f, h–k, q** and **Supplementary Fig. 3a, c**).
2. **`T CD8 CM` and `T CD8 CTL` depletion**: `T CD8 CM` mortality depletion was rescued by sample-size expansion ($\mathrm{AUC} = 0.28, P = 0.015, q = 0.073; P_{\mathrm{het}} = 0.70$; power `9% → 71%`), whereas `T CD8 CTL` contraction with severity ($\rho = -0.35, P = 0.0045, q = 0.019$; `14/14` variants) and death ($\mathrm{AUC} = 0.28, P = 0.014, q = 0.073$; `12/14` variants) reflected enriched Wave-1 pathology (`PBISC new` $\rho = -0.60$ vs. `Real` $\rho = -0.14, P_{\mathrm{het}} = 0.035$) backed by the highest single-donor `PBISC`–`Real` fidelity among all candidates (`shared ρ = +0.55, P = 0.0030`; **Fig. 7h, i, l–n, q**). Neither $\mathrm{CD8^+}$ subset separated mortality within `WHO 2–3` ($\mathrm{AUC} = 0.46$ and $0.30$).
3. **Severity-independent `HSPC` mortality mobilization**: `HSPC` discriminated fatal COVID-19 identically in `Real` ($\mathrm{AUC} = 0.81, P = 0.012$) and `PBISC new` ($\mathrm{AUC} = 0.80, P = 0.022; P_{\mathrm{het}} = 0.97$), achieving multi-test significance in `Real + PBISC new` ($\mathrm{AUC} = 0.81, P = 6.3 \times 10^{-4}, q = 0.014$; `13/14` variants; power `41% → 99%`, `n80: 31 → ≤ 10`; **Fig. 7f, h, i, o–q**). Doubling deaths resolved `Real`-only fragility under acute direct/indirect COVID-19 deaths (`5 → 11` deaths; $P = 0.13 \to 0.0066$) and direct COVID-19 deaths (`3 → 9` deaths; $P = 0.36 \to 0.019$), strengthened in samples `> day 7` ($\mathrm{AUC} = 0.86, P = 0.0011$), and uniquely among the `12` Step-4 subsets predicted death within `WHO 2–3` patients ($\mathrm{AUC} = 0.77, P = 0.015$) and in severity-stratified permutation testing ($\mathrm{AUC} = 0.77, P = 0.039$; **Fig. 7p**).

Across four independent adult COVID-19 single-cell PBMC cohorts ($N = 219$ acute patients, $23$ deaths; **Supplementary Fig. 1d** and **Supplementary Fig. 4**), uniform random-effects meta-analyses replicated all four severity anchors (`NK CD56bright` $\rho = -0.25, P = 0.0014$; `B ASC dividing` $\rho = +0.33, P = 5.2 \times 10^{-4}$; `T CD8 CM / EM` $\rho = -0.33, P = 0.0026$; `CD16 mono % Mono` $\rho = -0.35, P = 0.0093$), `B ASC total` severity expansion without mortality association ($\rho = +0.28, P = 0.0028$; death $\mathrm{AUC} = 0.46, P = 0.77$; `Stephenson` count-GLM `IgG` $\mathrm{OR} = 1.95, P = 0.0041$ and `IgA` $\mathrm{OR} = 1.84, P = 0.0025$), univariate `T CD8 CM / EM` mortality loss ($\mathrm{AUC} = 0.31, P = 0.038$; within `WHO-CPS ≥ 6` $\mathrm{AUC} = 0.42, P = 0.35$), Wave-1/hospitalized `T CD8 CTL` severity contraction (`COMBAT` $\rho = -0.31, P = 0.0067$; `van der Wijst` $\rho = -0.34, P = 0.030$), and `HSPC` severity ($\rho = +0.45, P = 2.8 \times 10^{-8}$) and overall / `> day 7` mortality elevation ($\mathrm{AUC} = 0.68\text{--}0.71, P \le 0.011$), while both candidates set aside in **Fig. 7h** (`NK CD56dim` and `CD16 mono C1`) remained null.

- **j–o**, Patient-level within-source `Normal score` distributions across WHO severity (**j**, `B ASC IgG`; **k**, `B ASC IgA`; **l**, `T CD8 CTL`) and survival (**m**, `T CD8 CM`; **n**, `T CD8 CTL`; **o**, `HSPC`).
