# External Public COVID-19 Single-Cell Replication of Harmonized Figure 7 (`ver8`) Claims

## 1. Datasets Audited (Zero Donor Overlap with ESKD or Across Cohorts)
We re-analyzed **four independent adult COVID-19 PBMC scRNA-seq / CITE-seq cohorts** (2,456,524 cells in the four annotation tables; 1,773,390 from COVID-19-positive samples before QC and acute-window filters) ($N = 219$ acute COVID-19 patients with WHO severity grading, $23$ fatal cases):
1. **Stephenson et al., *Nature Medicine* 2021** (UK 3-centre general-population CITE-seq: Newcastle, Cambridge, London; $n = 73$ acute COVID-19 patients with WHO-CPS grading, $6$ deaths, after excluding the documented `B_malignant` CLL donor `CV0198` under the identical CLL-exclusion rule applied in ESKD).
2. **COMBAT Consortium, *Cell* 2022** (Oxford Multi-omics Blood Atlas CITE-seq; $n = 75$ acute COVID-19 patients, $7$ deaths).
3. **van der Wijst et al., *Science Translational Medicine* 2021** (UCSF longitudinal hospitalized CITE-seq; $n = 42$ acute hospitalized COVID-19 patients, $7$ deaths).
4. **Liu et al., *Cell* 2021** (NIAID / Yale / Stanford time-resolved hospitalized CITE-seq; $n = 29$ complete innate+adaptive PBMC COVID-19 patients, $3$ deaths).

*(Note: Yoshida et al., Nature 2022 was audited separately and excluded from adult pooling because 11 adult donors directly overlap Stephenson 2021 and the remaining 13 acute donors are predominantly asymptomatic neonates/infants whose physiological neonatal hematopoiesis—high baseline `HSPC` and near-zero `T CD8 CM`—confounds cross-age severity correlation.)*

## 3. Verification notes (2026-11-18)

Numbers above were checked against `data/*.csv` and Supplementary Fig. 3; eight values were corrected and the cell count was reworded (total and Liu *N*, NK CD56bright meta *P*, Stephenson IgG/IgA Spearman, Stephenson T CD8 CM death AUC, van der Wijst T CD8 CTL severity and death, Stephenson T CD8 CTL ρ). Points to state in the Methods or legend:

- **Reproducibility.** Re-running `run_and_plot_public_replication_v8.py` on the original obs tables reproduced all four CSVs (max difference 3.4×10⁻¹⁴). An independent re-implementation (`verify_public_independent.py`) matched every cohort-level ρ, AUC, *P* and patient/death count and the random-effects meta-estimates. The figure title and legend now read *N* = 219 (`PBISC_SuppFig_S3_Public_COVID_Replication_20261118`).
- **AUC standard errors** use the Hanley–McNeil formula with a floor of 0.035; the meta-analysed death AUCs depend on that choice for cohorts with few deaths.

- **Cell-type proxies differ by cohort.** `T CD8 CM` in Stephenson is `CD8.EM` (that annotation has no CD8 central-memory label). `T CD8 CTL` is `CD8.TE` (Stephenson), `CD8.TEMRA + TEFF + TEM` (COMBAT), `T8_Mem` (van der Wijst) and `CD8 memory T cell` (Liu). `B ASC dividing` is `Plasmablast` in Stephenson and `PB_Prolif` in van der Wijst. COMBAT `B ASC total/switched` is the whole PB compartment.
- **Cohort sets differ by feature.** Liu 2021 is left out of the NK CD56bright and B ASC total meta-analyses although it has both features (and appears as a grey point in panel a). Including it gives NK CD56bright ρ = −0.25 (95% CI −0.39 to −0.10, *P* = 0.0014, *I*² = 19%) and B ASC total ρ = +0.28 (0.10 to 0.44, *P* = 0.0028, *I*² = 40%); conclusions are unchanged.
- **Panel a comparators are not always the same feature.** The ESKD square for "B ASC (mature/total)" is ESKD `B ASC IgG`, and the ESKD square for "CD16 mono (% Mono)" is ESKD CD16 mono as % of PBMCs.
- **Wording.** Stephenson is the only cohort with IgG/IgA plasma-cell labels, and there the Spearman tests are not significant (IgG *P* = 0.052, IgA *P* = 0.12); only the count GLM is. "Strongly replicated" fits B ASC total better than IgG/IgA separately. A null public result for NK CD56dim and CD16 mono C1 is consistent with, not proof of, the Fig. 7d exclusions.
