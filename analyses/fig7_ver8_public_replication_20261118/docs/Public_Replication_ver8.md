# External Public COVID-19 Single-Cell Replication of Harmonized Figure 7 (`ver8`) Findings (Reflected & Synchronized, 2026-11-18)

## 1. Datasets Audited (Zero Donor Overlap with ESKD or Across Cohorts)
We re-analyzed **four independent adult COVID-19 PBMC scRNA-seq / CITE-seq cohorts** (`2,456,524` cells in the four annotation tables; `1,773,390` from COVID-19-positive samples before QC and acute-window filters; **$N = 219$ acute COVID-19 patients with WHO severity grading, $23$ fatal cases**):
1. **Stephenson et al., *Nature Medicine* 2021** (UK 3-centre general-population CITE-seq: Newcastle, Cambridge, London; $n = 73$ acute COVID-19 patients with WHO-CPS grading, $6$ deaths, after excluding the documented `B_malignant` CLL donor `CV0198` under the identical CLL-exclusion rule applied in ESKD).
2. **COMBAT Consortium, *Cell* 2022** (Oxford Multi-omics Blood Atlas Wave-1 CITE-seq; $n = 75$ acute COVID-19 patients, $7$ deaths).
3. **van der Wijst et al., *Science Translational Medicine* 2021** (UCSF longitudinal hospitalized CITE-seq; $n = 42$ acute hospitalized COVID-19 patients, $7$ deaths).
4. **Liu et al., *Cell* 2021** (NIAID / Yale / Stanford time-resolved hospitalized CITE-seq; $n = 29$ complete innate+adaptive PBMC acute COVID-19 patients, $3$ deaths).

*(Note: Yoshida et al., Nature 2022 was audited separately and excluded from adult pooling because 11 adult donors directly overlap Stephenson 2021 and the remaining 13 acute donors are predominantly asymptomatic neonates/infants whose physiological neonatal hematopoiesis—high baseline `HSPC` and near-zero `T CD8 CM`—confounds cross-age severity correlation.)*

## 2. Updates Reflected in Supplementary Figure 3 (`PBISC_SuppFig_S3_Public_COVID_Replication_20261118`)

All verification items from `README_Public_Replication_ver8_corrected.md` have been incorporated directly into both the statistical pipeline (`run_and_plot_public_replication_v8.py`, `data/*.csv`) and `Supplementary Figure 3`:
1. **Exact Sample Size (`N = 219`)**: Cohort total corrected from `222` to **`N = 219`** (`73 + 75 + 42 + 29`, `23` deaths) across the figure title, legend, and tables.
2. **Uniform Cohort Sets Across Meta-Analyses**: Every cohort resolving a given feature is now pooled uniformly, with `(k, N)` displayed on every row of Panels `a` and `c`. Specifically, `Liu 2021` ($n = 29$) is included in both `NK CD56bright` ($k = 4, N = 219$: $\rho = -0.25, P = 0.0014$) and `B ASC total` ($k = 4, N = 219$: $\rho = +0.28, P = 0.0028$), matching the plotted individual-cohort grey markers one-to-one.
3. **Exact Feature-Matched ESKD Comparators in Panel `a`**:
   - Public `B ASC total` is paired with ESKD **`B ASC total`** (sum of all five `B_ASC` subsets: $\rho = +0.44, P = 1.8 \times 10^{-4}$) rather than ESKD `B ASC IgG` alone.
   - Public `CD16 mono (% Mono)` is paired with ESKD **`CD16 mono (% Mono)`** (`CD16mono|lin`: $\rho = -0.52, P = 9.3 \times 10^{-6}$) rather than `% of PBMCs` ($\rho = -0.40, P = 8.6 \times 10^{-4}$; `% PBMC` public meta $\rho = -0.16, P = 0.027$ is also archived in `data/public_meta_analysis_v8.csv`).
4. **Transparent Dual Display for `Stephenson 2021` Isotypes in Panel `b`**: Because `Stephenson 2021` ($n = 73$) is the only public cohort resolving `Plasma cell IgG` and `IgA` isotypes—and discrete droplet zero-ties render unadjusted Spearman $\rho$ borderline/non-significant (`IgG` $\rho = +0.23, P = 0.052$; `IgA` $\rho = +0.19, P = 0.12$) whereas age/sex/site-adjusted quasi-binomial count-GLMs are significant (`IgG` $\mathrm{OR} = 1.95, P = 0.0041$; `IgA` $\mathrm{OR} = 1.84, P = 0.0025$)—Panel `b` now shows **both** side-by-side (`Spearman ρ (% PBMC)` with open markers for $P \ge 0.05$ on the left; `Adj. count-GLM OR / step` on the right).
5. **Explicit Cell-Type Proxy Footnote & Both Unfollowed Candidates (`NK CD56dim` & `CD16 mono C1`)**: Panels `a` and `c` include both `NK CD56dim` ($k = 4$) and `CD16 mono C1` ($k = 1$, `Stephenson`) as candidates not followed in Fig. 7d, mark proxy-mapped labels with `*`, and document exact per-cohort annotation proxies in the figure footnote and legend, alongside the Hanley–McNeil $\mathrm{AUC}$ SE specification (`SE` floor `0.035`).
