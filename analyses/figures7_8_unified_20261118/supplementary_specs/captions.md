All 43 leukocyte-label associations. Patient scores are equal-visit means followed by within-source rankINT; the full fixed43 family is retained in Real37/New29/Combined66. Coverage of every common label and complete primary RNA screen. Patients require at least three cells of the label across all eligible visits. Eligibility additionally requires ≥10 patients, ≥3 deaths and ≥3 survivors in each source; nine labels qualify and seven satisfy the separately defined rarity criterion. Counts in panel b are Combined gene tests passing q<.05, including every qualifying label and all null counts. Local BH uses5,762 common symbols within each label/endpoint; global BH uses9×5,762=51,858 per endpoint. No Combined test passes the global joint103,716 family. Scores are patient cell-weighted means of log1p(CP10K) over the shared genes and source-wise rankINT. Coverage failures are not biological negative findings, and rare-subset filenames do not make every label rare.

Source-specific Tγδ ADK severity and MXRA7 mortality associations with50,000 fixed-score patient-bootstrap95% CIs. ## Supplementary_Figure_S4

Bulk KIAA0391 context in exactly the same29 New patients and79 eligible draws, and all-visit shared-patient HSPC scores in24 patients. Severity is recoded as0=mild,1=moderate,2=severe,3=critical, not the WHO0–8 clinical progression scale. Bulk values are draw-level log2(CPM+1), using all60,605 unique Ensembl rows in the denominator, then averaged with equal draw weights within patient. HSPC values are cell-weighted patient means of log1p(CP10K) normalized over the shared5,762-symbol universe. Panel a uses deterministic horizontal jitter solely to show tied ordinal severity; no statistic is recomputed from jittered coordinates. Panel b compares different bulk and inferred-cell estimands. Panel c is not a same-day validation: exact lag data are not available in the plotted package. Correlations/P are existing descriptive canonical statistics; no CI was available for these scatter summaries. Non-significance does not establish an absent bulk effect, cell specificity, or superiority; negative shared concordance does not by itself distinguish biological timing from reconstruction error.
